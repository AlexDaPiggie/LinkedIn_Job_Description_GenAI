## About the Role 
 
 As an AI Engineer Intern at Alex AI, you will play a pivotal role in building, monitoring, and evaluating AI systems designed to generate high-quality LinkedIn job descriptions. This is an exceptional opportunity to gain hands-on experience in AI engineering, work on real-world projects, and contribute to the development of impactful AI solutions. You will collaborate closely with our team of experts, learning from their extensive knowledge and expertise while making a tangible impact on our products.
 
## Responsibilities 
 
- Monitor and maintain the performance of machine learning pipelines to ensure optimal functionality and efficiency.
- Evaluate the quality of model outputs, providing insights and recommendations for continuous improvement.
- Prepare and clean training data to enhance the accuracy and reliability of our AI models.
- Support the testing of prompts and the comparison of different models to identify the most effective solutions.
 
## Requirements 
 
- Proficient in Python programming, with a strong understanding of its applications in machine learning and data analysis.
- Experience with SQL for data manipulation and querying, ensuring data integrity and accessibility.
- Foundational knowledge of machine learning concepts and techniques, with a focus on practical applications.
- Hands-on experience working with data pipelines, including data ingestion, processing, and storage.
 
## Nice to Have
