# AI Engineer Intern
 
## About Alex AI 
 
 Alex AI is an innovative startup dedicated to revolutionizing the creation of hiring content through advanced AI solutions. We empower companies to produce exceptional job descriptions and other recruitment materials with cutting-edge technology.
 
## About the Role 
 
 As an AI Engineer Intern at Alex AI, you will play a crucial role in the development, monitoring, and evaluation of our sophisticated AI systems. You will directly contribute to building AI that generates high-quality LinkedIn job descriptions, gaining invaluable hands-on experience in a fast-paced startup environment.
 
## Responsibilities 
 
- Actively monitor the performance and health of machine learning pipelines, ensuring optimal operation.
- Rigorously evaluate the quality of model outputs, providing critical feedback for continuous improvement.
- Prepare, clean, and preprocess datasets to ensure they are suitable for training and evaluating AI models.
- Support comprehensive prompt testing initiatives and conduct comparative analyses of model performance to identify areas for enhancement.
 
## Requirements 
 
- Proficiency in Python programming for data manipulation, scripting, and model development.
- Solid understanding and practical experience with SQL for data querying and management.
- Foundational knowledge of machine learning concepts, algorithms, and principles.
- Demonstrated experience working with and contributing to data pipelines.
 
## Nice to Have 
 
- Familiarity with system design principles and experience contributing to architectural discussions.
- Experience with Figma for UI/UX design collaboration.
- Exposure to frontend development principles and technologies.
- Understanding of signal processing techniques and their applications.
 
## Benefits 
 
- Comprehensive health insurance coverage.
- Daily catered meals, including breakfast and lunch.
- Invaluable hands-on experience in AI engineering and a cutting-edge tech startup.
- Opportunities for professional growth and skill development in a dynamic environment.
 
## Why Join Us 
 
 This is a unique opportunity to join a dynamic and rapidly growing startup where your contributions will have a tangible and visible impact. You'll work with real-world AI systems, experience rapid learning, and be an integral part of a team pushing the boundaries of AI in hiring.
