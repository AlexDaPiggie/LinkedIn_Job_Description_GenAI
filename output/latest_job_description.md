# AI Engineer
 
## About AlexAI 
 
 AlexAI is an innovative startup dedicated to transforming how organizations communicate with potential talent. By building modern tools that leverage artificial intelligence, AlexAI empowers companies and recruiting teams to craft higher-quality, more engaging, and effective hiring content. Our platform simplifies complex workflow challenges for small recruiting teams, allowing them to optimize candidate reach, improve candidate engagement, and streamline content creation. As an agile and fast-growing team, we value innovation, clarity, collaborative execution, and building meaningful solutions that solve everyday challenges for hiring professionals.
 
## About the Role 
 
 We are seeking a dedicated AI Engineer to lead the planning and delivery for our growing hiring platform. In this role, you will serve as a central driver in shaping the platform's execution, bridging the gap between platform strategy, creative design, and technical execution. You will play a pivotal role in serving small recruiting teams who rely on our platform to elevate their recruitment efforts. This position offers a unique blend of technical leadership, platform planning, and hands-on execution within a collaborative startup environment, allowing you to take end-to-end ownership of core delivery initiatives from concept through production.
 
## Responsibilities 
 
- Lead the formal definition and documentation of clear, detailed product and platform requirements to guide feature delivery and platform enhancements.
- Coordinate daily workflows, timelines, and deliverables across cross-functional design and engineering teams to ensure seamless project execution and alignment.
- Monitor, analyze, and synthesize key product metrics alongside direct user feedback to evaluate platform performance, inform future prioritization, and continually refine the platform experience.
 
## Requirements 
 
- Demonstrated experience in product management or equivalent role focused on planning, driving, and delivering software product features.
- Strong written communication skills with a proven track record of creating clear documentation, requirements, and cross-team updates.
- High comfort level and proven effectiveness working closely with cross-functional teams in a collaborative, fast-paced environment.
 
## Nice to Have 
 
- Practical understanding or prior exposure to foundational system design principles.
- Familiarity with Figma or similar collaborative design tools for reviewing design files and user interface prototypes.
- Background, exposure, or baseline technical experience in frontend development practices.
- Knowledge of or background in signal processing principles.
 
## Benefits 
 
- Comprehensive health insurance coverage to support your personal wellness.
- Fully covered daily meals including breakfast, lunch, and dinner provided on-site.
- Direct, hands-on experience building and deploying real-world AI engineering solutions in an active product environment.
 
## Why Join Us 
 
 At AlexAI, you will step directly into a fast-moving, high-impact startup environment where your contributions are immediately visible and valued. We offer a culture that prioritizes rapid learning, direct ownership, and meaningful practical application. Whether working on real AI systems or leading cross-functional platform efforts, you will have the unique opportunity to learn quickly alongside an ambitious team while directly shaping tools that redefine how small recruiting teams operate and hire.
