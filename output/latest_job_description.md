# AI Engineer Intern
 
## About Alex AI 
 
 Alex AI is a dynamic startup dedicated to revolutionizing the hiring process by developing innovative AI tools that empower companies to create compelling and effective job descriptions. Our mission is to leverage cutting-edge technology to enhance the recruitment experience for both employers and job seekers.
 
## About the Role 
 
 As an AI Engineer Intern at Alex AI, you will play a pivotal role in building, monitoring, and evaluating AI systems designed to generate high-quality LinkedIn job descriptions. This is an exciting opportunity to contribute to real-world AI projects, gain hands-on experience, and make a tangible impact in a fast-paced startup environment.
 
## Responsibilities 
 
- Monitor and maintain machine learning pipelines to ensure optimal performance and reliability.
- Evaluate the quality of model outputs and provide insights to improve AI-generated job descriptions.
- Prepare and clean training data to enhance the accuracy and effectiveness of our AI models.
- Support the testing of various prompts and facilitate model comparisons to identify the most effective solutions.
 
## Requirements 
 
- Proficient in Python programming for data manipulation and analysis.
- Strong SQL skills for database management and querying.
- Foundational knowledge of machine learning concepts and techniques.
- Experience working with data pipelines to streamline data flow and processing.
 
## Nice to Have 
 
- Familiarity with system design principles to architect scalable and efficient solutions.
- Experience with Figma for designing user interfaces and improving user experience.
- Basic knowledge of frontend development to enhance the integration of AI models with web applications.
- Understanding of signal processing techniques to improve data analysis and model performance.

- Comprehensive health insurance coverage to support your well-being.

- Hands-on AI engineering experience to accelerate your learning and career growth.

## Why Join Us 
 
 Join a fast-moving startup where interns can work on real AI systems, learn quickly, and have a visible impact. This is your chance to make a difference in the hiring landscape and gain invaluable experience in the AI field.
