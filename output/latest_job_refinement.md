# AI Engineer Intern
 
## About Alex AI 
 
 Alex AI is a startup revolutionizing hiring content creation with cutting-edge AI. We help companies generate high-quality job descriptions and other hiring materials, streamlining recruitment and enhancing employer branding.
 
## About the Role 
 
 As an AI Engineer Intern at Alex AI, you'll develop, maintain, and assess our AI systems. This internship offers hands-on experience in a fast-paced startup, contributing directly to AI tools that generate impactful job descriptions. You'll ensure the quality and efficiency of our ML pipelines and model outputs.
 
## Responsibilities 
 
- Monitor machine learning pipelines for performance and health, identifying issues.
- Evaluate model outputs for quality and accuracy, providing feedback for improvement.
- Prepare, clean, and preprocess datasets for AI model training and evaluation.
- Participate in prompt testing and analyze model outputs to enhance system capabilities.
- Contribute to the development and refinement of AI models for professional content generation.
 
## Requirements 
 
- Proficiency in Python for scripting, data manipulation, and AI/ML development.
- Strong understanding and experience with SQL for data querying.
- Solid knowledge of machine learning concepts, algorithms, and best practices.
- Experience with data pipelines, including construction, monitoring, and optimization.
 
## Nice to Have 
 
- Familiarity with system design principles.
- Experience with design and prototyping tools like Figma.
- Exposure to frontend development technologies.
- Understanding of signal processing techniques.
 
## Benefits 
 
- Comprehensive health insurance.
- Daily catered meals.
- Hands-on AI engineering experience in a startup.
- Opportunities for professional development and mentorship.
