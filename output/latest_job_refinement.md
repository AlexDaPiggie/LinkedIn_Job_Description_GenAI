# AI Engineer
 
## About AlexAI 
 
 Alex AI is an innovative startup dedicated to developing tools that empower organizations to create better hiring content through the use of artificial intelligence. Our mission is to enhance the recruiting process, making it more efficient and effective for small teams.
 
## About the Role 
 
 As an AI Engineer, you will play a crucial role in leading the planning and delivery of our rapidly growing hiring platform, aimed specifically at small recruiting teams. This position requires a proactive approach to product management and a focus on collaboration. Your expertise will be essential in transforming ideas into tangible features that meet user needs and drive the business forward.
 
## Responsibilities 
 
- Define and articulate detailed product requirements to ensure alignment with business goals and user needs.
- Coordinate design and engineering efforts to deliver high-quality product features on schedule.
- Review and analyze product metrics and user feedback to inform continuous improvement and strategic decisions.
 
## Requirements 
 
- Demonstrated experience in product management, showcasing the ability to lead cross-functional initiatives.
- Excellent written communication skills to create clear documentation and present ideas effectively.
- A collaborative mindset with a proven ability to work effectively with cross-functional teams, ensuring successful project delivery.
 
## Nice to Have 
 
- Experience with system design principles to contribute to scalable and maintainable solutions.
- Familiarity with design tools such as Figma to aid in the visualization of product concepts.
- Basic knowledge of frontend development to facilitate better communication with engineering teams.
- Understanding of signal processing concepts that can enhance AI capabilities in recruitment.
 
## Benefits 
 
- Comprehensive health insurance plans to support your well-being.
- Daily meals including lunch, breakfast, and dinner, ensuring you stay nourished throughout the workday.
- Opportunities for hands-on AI engineering experience, allowing you to work on impactful projects and enhance your skills.
 
## Why Join Us 
 
 Join Alex AI to immerse yourself in a fast-moving startup environment where your contributions directly impact real AI systems. This role offers a unique opportunity for rapid learning and the chance to make a visible difference in the recruitment landscape. You'll collaborate with talented individuals and gain invaluable experience that will accelerate your career in the tech industry.
