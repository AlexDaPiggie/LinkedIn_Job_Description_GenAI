# AI job description generator competitors and generator UX (as of Nov 2026)

Method note: ~17 searches/fetches. Several vendor pages blocked (403: Betterteam, PeopleManagingPeople). Many listicle sources are vendor-authored and self-promotional; flagged where relevant. No conversion data by flow type was found.

## 1. Which comparable products exist, and what do they offer (features, gating, input flow, output, export, editing)?

### Takeaway
Two camps. (a) Free, top-of-funnel standalone generators (Workable, Grammarly, Ongig GPT, Testlify, Recooty, HiveDesk, Pitchnhire etc.) - title in, draft out, copy-paste. (b) JD writers embedded in paid ATS/job platforms (LinkedIn Hiring Pro, Indeed, Greenhouse, Ashby, Workable, Teamtailor, Recruiterflow) - gated behind a plan/posting. Textio is the premium scoring/bias layer. Few free tools offer upload-existing-JD autofill, Word export, or structured LinkedIn-formatted output, which is our niche.

### Cited Findings
**LinkedIn (Hiring Pro / job post AI)**
- Hiring Pro drafts a JD "based on the role" via a "Start hiring with AI" button when posting a job; rolling out gradually, desktop only, limited to a subset of members who promote their jobs; user must review/edit; can opt out via classic experience. - [LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/learning/answer/a6895976)
- LinkedIn business page says the AI assistant generates JDs, screening questions, and candidate qualifications; English only; requires promoting the job for features like candidate search. - [LinkedIn Business](https://business.linkedin.com/in/en/hire/post-jobs/ai-assistant) (via search summary)
- Hiring Assistant (Oct 2024 launch) can turn recruiter notes or reference postings into a JD, and does sourcing/outreach. - [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/29/linkedin-launches-its-first-ai-agent-to-take-on-the-role-of-job-recruiters), [People Matters](https://www.peoplematters.in/article/recruitment/everything-you-need-to-know-about-linkedins-new-hiring-assistant-tool-43265). No 2026 page confirming current feature list/pricing found.
- Implication: LinkedIn's own tool is login-gated, only inside the posting flow, no standalone/export. - inference

**Indeed AI JD tool**
- Inputs: job title, number of hires, role type; either one-shot full draft or step-by-step with tips; accept/reject suggested titles and wording; GPT plus Indeed's own job data. Draft in <5s in beta. - [Indeed](https://uk.indeed.com/lead/attract-great-candidates-with-indeeds-new-ai-powered-job-description-tool)
- Claims 750k+ employers, ~2M jobs, ~16% average increase in positive connections (Indeed's own data, unverified). Free for employers posting directly on Indeed in US/UK/CA/FR. Same source.
- Indeed also cites HRSG research that writing a JD can take 2+ hours. Same source.

**Workable**
- Standalone free JD generator, no signup per Workable help center; wizard uses role categories/title. - [Workable help](https://help.workable.com/hc/en-us/articles/18602440465815), [Metaview roundup](https://www.metaview.ai/resources/blog/free-ai-job-description-generators)
- In-product AI writer outputs role summary, responsibilities, requirements, benefits; adapts tone to seniority/department; suggests inclusive language and flags biased phrasing (depth unexplained); available across standard plans; 15-day trial mentioned. - [Workable guide](https://resources.workable.com/inside-hr/workable-ai-features-the-complete-guide-for-recruiters/)
- Conflict: one directory labels it "Paid" - [neura.market](https://neura.market/directories/ai-tools/job-description-generator-by-workable); Workable's own help says free/no login. Treat as free standalone.

**Textio**
- Drafts job posts from a few details, reusable JD library/templates, brand-voice guidance; Textio Score grades predicted applicant appeal; gender meter flags skewed language and suggests fixes; age graph; extensions for ATS/HRIS/LinkedIn; no public pricing, "Book a demo". - [Textio](https://textio.com/products/flow)
- Pricing is third-party estimates and conflicting: ToolRadar lists Standard at $6,257/6 months (up to 200 posts); others say ~$12k-15k/yr. Unverified. - [ToolRadar via search](https://toolradar.com/tools/textio)

**Greenhouse**
- Enter title/qualifications/responsibilities, AI drafts; options: save (replace/append), regenerate, adjust tone, discard. States output is NOT automatically bias-checked. Job admin+, Core/Plus/Pro, admin must enable. - [Greenhouse support](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/36960585376283-Job-post-description-suggestions) (conflict: [Greenhouse AI features page](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/33043749845403-Greenhouse-AI-features) reportedly says all tiers)

**Ashby**
- AI Content Assistant inside rich text editor; can use existing job postings as context to build new JDs; available to all customers. - [Ashby](https://www.ashbyhq.com/product-updates/the-new-ai-content-assistant-uses-rich-context-to-help-you-generate-content-direct-in-ashby)

**Teamtailor**
- No dedicated JD generator found; Co-pilot edits/transforms text in Job Editor with built-in actions + custom prompts; admin must activate. - [Teamtailor](https://teamtailor.com/en/productnews/co-pilot-can-now-edit-text-your-way)

**Ongig**
- Free "generator" is a ChatGPT custom GPT: job title only, copy/paste out, no file export; bias detection, readability scoring, custom templates, approvals, localization are in paid Text Analyzer platform; page leans on case studies, ATS/job-board partners, press, testimonials. - [Ongig](https://www.ongig.com/free-ai-job-description-generator)

**Grammarly**
- Free, prompt-based: describe role (company, location, type, bullets) then adjust formality/tone/length. Needs a (free) Grammarly account per one summary; Metaview says no account. Conflicting. No hiring-specific context. - [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com/ai/ai-writing-tools/job-description-generator), [Metaview](https://www.metaview.ai/resources/blog/free-ai-job-description-generators)

**Metaview**
- Free with work email; drafts JD from recruiter/hiring-manager intake call, ties to competency rubric/scorecard; ATS write-back; multilingual. - [Metaview](https://www.metaview.ai/resources/blog/free-ai-job-description-generators) (vendor-authored)

**ChatGPT / Claude**
- Custom GPT or Project per role family seeded with past JDs; free tiers have message caps; copy-paste only. - [Metaview](https://www.metaview.ai/resources/blog/free-ai-job-description-generators)

**Betterteam**
- Not an AI generator in what I found: 2,000+ static templates/sample JDs (Word download), posts to 100+ job boards (paid). Strong SEO template library pattern. - [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/features/30-resources-to-find-job-description-examples), [Betterteam](https://www.betterteam.com/job-description-template) (page blocked; via search snippet)

**Others (thin evidence)**
- Flair (HR wizard: title, language, salary, location, seniority, education) - [Flair](https://flair.hr/en/whats-new/flair-ai-job-descriptions); Recruiterflow (generate then edit in ATS) - [Recruiterflow](https://recruiterflow.com/ai-job-description-generator); Testlify (title, industry, language, company; "no sign-up"), Hireguide (JD + interview kit, free starter), Skima, Recooty - via [Metaview](https://www.metaview.ai/resources/blog/free-ai-job-description-generators)/search snippets.

### Inferences
- Shortlist of 10 comparables for the portfolio: LinkedIn Hiring Pro, Indeed, Workable, Textio, Greenhouse, Ashby, Ongig GPT, Grammarly, Betterteam (template SEO), Hireguide/Metaview (kit/intake-adjacent). ChatGPT custom GPTs as the "substitute" baseline.
- Our differentiators vs the field: no login, upload-existing-JD autofill, LinkedIn-specific formatting, Word export. None of the sources show competitors doing all four.

### Gaps
- Not verified: Juicebox, Gem, Pinpoint, HireBase, SmartRecruiters, Recruitee, Canva/Notion JD generators, Jobscan (search returned nothing relevant; Jobscan is candidate-side).
- No G2/Capterra/Product Hunt review text retrieved for any JD tool (Capterra/Gartner pages for Textio surfaced but not read).
- Betterteam pricing and any generator not confirmed (page 403).
- Current (2026) paid tiers for LinkedIn Hiring Pro not found.

## 2. Which input flows (wizard vs chat vs single prompt) convert best for recruiters?

### Takeaway
No conversion data by flow type found. Observed market pattern: title-first minimal input with instant draft dominates free tools; richer ATS tools offer optional step-by-step. Indeed offers both modes explicitly.

### Cited Findings
- Minimal input (title only) in Ongig GPT; title + industry/language/company in Testlify; title + few details in Workable/Greenhouse. - sources above
- Indeed offers "complete post from a few inputs" OR "step by step with tips at each stage". - [Indeed](https://uk.indeed.com/lead/attract-great-candidates-with-indeeds-new-ai-powered-job-description-tool)
- Workable's standalone is a wizard selecting predefined role categories. - [Metaview](https://www.metaview.ai/resources/blog/free-ai-job-description-generator)
- LinkedIn Hiring Assistant accepts rough notes or reference postings. - [People Matters](https://www.peoplematters.in/article/recruitment/everything-you-need-to-know-about-linkedins-new-hiring-assistant-tool-43265)
- Recruiter advice: first align internally on the role, then use AI; Visconti writes own JD then merges with AI version to catch missed skills. - [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/09/30/recruiters-caution-against-using-ai-write-job-postings-been-trained-on-crappy-descriptions/), [IT Brew](https://www.itbrew.com/stories/2025/09/29/recruiting-against-using-ai-job-descriptions)

### Inferences
- Our guided-question wizard is defensible because it forces role-specific input (counters the "generic" complaint). Offer a fast path (title-only/quick draft, or upload JD) plus optional depth - mirrors Indeed's dual mode.
- "Paste/upload your own JD and improve it" matches the Visconti behaviour and Ashby's "use existing postings as context".

### Gaps
- No A/B or conversion stats on wizard vs chat vs single prompt. Would need first-party testing.

## 3. Common output extras (inclusive score, pay range, readability, SEO, multi-format, screening/interview kit, variants)?

### Takeaway
Inclusive/bias scoring is premium (Textio, Ongig paid). Free tools mostly give plain draft plus tone/length knobs. Interview kits/scorecards and screening questions appear as adjacent features. Pay range is increasingly legally required, so a salary field is high value.

### Cited Findings
- Textio: score, gender meter, age graph - [Textio](https://textio.com/products/flow). Ongig: bias detection, readability only in paid platform - [Ongig](https://www.ongig.com/free-ai-job-description-generator).
- Greenhouse explicitly says no automatic bias check - [Greenhouse](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/36960585376283-Job-post-description-suggestions). Workable claims inclusive-language suggestions - [Workable](https://resources.workable.com/inside-hr/workable-ai-features-the-complete-guide-for-recruiters/).
- Tone/length/regenerate controls: Greenhouse (tone, regenerate), Grammarly (formality, tone, length). Same sources.
- LinkedIn AI assistant also generates screening questions and qualifications - [LinkedIn Business](https://business.linkedin.com/in/en/hire/post-jobs/ai-assistant). Hireguide pairs JD with interview kit; Metaview with scorecard - [Metaview](https://www.metaview.ai/resources/blog/free-ai-job-description-generators).
- Pay transparency: EU Directive transposition deadline 7 June 2026 (employers must give initial pay or range in ads; no pay-history questions), though most states missed the deadline - [Lewis Silkin](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2026/07/01/eu-pay-transparency-directive-2026-employer-compliance), [remoteworkeurope](https://remoteworkeurope.eu/news/2026/eu-pay-transparency-most-states-miss-deadline/). Virginia requires pay range in postings from 1 July 2026; California/Ontario revised range rules Jan 2026 - [Asanify](https://asanify.com/blog/news/virginia-salary-history-ban-june-25-2026/). ~24% of ads in states with rules were non-compliant as of Jan 2025 - [Deel](https://www.deel.com/deel-works/pay-transparency-compliance-gap-2026).
- Indeed gives accept/reject per suggested wording - granular control. - [Indeed](https://uk.indeed.com/lead/attract-great-candidates-with-indeeds-new-ai-powered-job-description-tool)

### Inferences
- Cheap wins for us: free inclusive-language/gender-coded word check (Textio-lite), readability score, pay-range completeness warning ("add range - required in CA/NY/VA/EU"), tone variants, screening questions, LinkedIn character-limit/format check.
- SEO/job-board keyword features: no competitor evidence found beyond Indeed using its job data for title/wording suggestions.

### Gaps
- No verified data on which extras recruiters actually use or pay for. SEO keyword features unconfirmed for any tool.

## 4. What do recruiters complain about with AI JDs?

### Takeaway
Generic, not company/team specific; trained on bad existing JDs; managers skip thinking about the role; too many bullets, no "why us", missing salary/benefits; unreviewed output; candidates suspect AI/copy-paste.

### Cited Findings
- "AI has been training on crappy job descriptions" (Paul DeBettignies); risk of more transactional hiring. - [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/09/30/recruiters-caution-against-using-ai-write-job-postings-been-trained-on-crappy-descriptions/), [IT Brew](https://www.itbrew.com/stories/2025/09/29/recruiting-against-using-ai-job-descriptions)
- Katrina Collier: output often not tailored to company/team; 66% of managers are "accidental" (Fortune, 2024 figure); managers dislike recruiting so accept AI output. Same sources.
- When asked why candidates decline, AI typically answers: too many bullets, no reason to work at company, too little salary/benefits info (DeBettignies). Same sources.
- Reddit themes via Ongig summary: vague by design, outdated, disconnect from hiring manager (key skill left off posting), AI/template suspicion, no proofreading with the hiring manager. - [Ongig](https://blog.ongig.com/?p=66977) (secondary summary, not primary Reddit threads)
- Greenhouse's own docs warn of unchecked bias. - [Greenhouse](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/36960585376283-Job-post-description-suggestions)
- Robert Half stat (32% rewrite JDs to discourage AI applications) appears in a low-quality aggregator, unverified - [search result summary](https://www.ismartrecruit.com/blogs/ai-generated-job-descriptions-impact-employer-branding); not relied on.

### Inferences
- Product responses: ask for company-specific "why join" + team context in the wizard; cap bullet counts; require pay/benefits fields; show "assumptions made / please verify" flags to counter hallucinated requirements; limit length options.
- Hallucinated requirements and length complaints: no direct primary source found.

### Gaps
- No primary Reddit (r/recruiting, r/humanresources) threads or G2/Capterra review text retrieved; hallucinated-requirements complaint not directly sourced.

## 5. Landing page / sub-page patterns that work (templates, role pages, examples, SEO) and trust/onboarding

### Takeaway
Winners pair a no-friction tool page with a large library of role-specific template/sample pages (Betterteam 2,000+), downloadable Word versions, and data-backed claims. Our existing role template pages (front_end/templates/*) match this pattern.

### Cited Findings
- Betterteam: 2,000+ templates, sample JDs downloadable in Word, posts to 100+ boards (paid upsell) - [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/features/30-resources-to-find-job-description-examples), [Betterteam](https://www.betterteam.com/job-description-template)
- Ongig tool page trust stack: case studies, clients, ATS/job-board partners, press, testimonials, accessibility page, newsletter, link to comparison blog - [Ongig](https://www.ongig.com/free-ai-job-description-generator)
- Indeed trust signals: employer count, jobs count, applicant lift % (self-reported) - [Indeed](https://uk.indeed.com/lead/attract-great-candidates-with-indeeds-new-ai-powered-job-description-tool)
- Onboarding friction ladder: no account (Workable, Testlify, Grammarly per Metaview) < free account (Grammarly per other source) < work email (Metaview) < paid plan + admin enablement (Greenhouse) < promoted job (LinkedIn) < demo (Textio). - sources above
- Many "best free generator" listicles (Metaview, Fitgap, Skima, Carv, ClickUp) rank free/no-signup tools - discoverability channel; [Fitgap](https://us.fitgap.com/search/ai-job-description-generator-tools/free)

### Inferences
- Opportunity: get listed in these roundups; add "no signup, Word export" in H1/subhead; show sample output above the fold; role pages with real example JD and CTA into pre-filled wizard.
- Self-reported lift stats are standard; a small product should use honest alternatives (time saved, number generated, a real sample).

### Gaps
- No landing-page conversion benchmarks; Betterteam, Canva, Notion pages not inspected directly.

## Synthesis: shared patterns, what recruiters value, opportunities

- Shared by best tools: fast first draft (<5s), human review gate ("nothing publishes without approval"), editable output, regenerate/tone controls, role/seniority-aware sections (summary, responsibilities, requirements, benefits), integration into where posting happens. - Indeed, Greenhouse, Workable sources
- Recruiters value: specificity to company/team, pay/benefits present, concise structure, time saved (JD takes 2+ hours per HRSG via Indeed), trust that a human reviewed it.
- Gaps a small product can exploit (inferences):
  1. No-login + export (Word/markdown/LinkedIn-ready) - LinkedIn tool is gated and non-portable; Ongig is copy-paste only.
  2. Upload-existing-JD improvement with a diff/"what changed and why".
  3. Free lightweight bias/readability/pay-transparency checks (Textio is demo-priced; Greenhouse has none).
  4. Anti-generic design: mandatory company-specific inputs, flagged assumptions, "why join" prompt.
  5. LinkedIn-specific formatting guidance (limits, structure), since LinkedIn's own AI is limited to promoted posts on desktop.
  6. Role-specific template pages for SEO feeding the wizard.
