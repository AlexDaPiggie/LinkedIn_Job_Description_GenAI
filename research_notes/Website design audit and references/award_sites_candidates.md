# Award / praised site candidates (ranked for vibe fit)
Research date 2026-11-16. Tools gave page TEXT only (no CSS), so fonts/colours come from Typewolf listings or memory.
Legend: V-exist = fetched, domain live. V-typewolf = Typewolf Site of the Day listing fetched (fonts verified). MEM = memory, award/colour claims NOT verified.
Typewolf index: https://www.typewolf.com/site-of-the-day (pages 1-3 fetched).

1. Every - https://every.to - V-typewolf SOTD 28 Sep 2025. Signifier (serif) + Switzer (sans). Editorial cards, stamp-like illustrations. Colours: MEM warm off-white. Study: serif display + neutral sans on a publication that also ships products ("Built by Every" cards).
2. Delphi - https://www.delphi.co (delphi.ai redirects) - V-typewolf SOTD 14 May 2025. Martina Plantijn (serif) + Inter. V-exist: chat preview, expert cards, filter pills. Study: real chat/input UI shown under a serif headline.
3. Speakeasy - https://www.speakeasy.com - V-typewolf SOTD 8 Dec 2025. Tobias (serif) + Diatype + Diatype Mono. Dev tool. Study: serif display with mono labels for technical precision.
4. Copper - (domain unconfirmed, find via Typewolf page 3) - V-typewolf SOTD 15 May 2025. Martina Plantijn + Monument Grotesk + Monument Grotesk Mono. Not confirmed as software.
5. Granola - https://www.granola.ai - V-exist. Notes app; shows notepad, "Enhanced" tab, chat chips. Fonts/colours MEM (serif headline, calm light UI). Study: product UI as hero, document-like notepad.
6. Attio - https://attio.com - V-exist. CRM; table, pipeline, workflow, email composer shown. Fonts MEM (clean sans, restrained). Study: dense real product screens, precise feel.
7. Mercury - https://mercury.com - V-exist. Banking for startups; virtual card, invoice, search UI shown. Awwwards inspiration page exists (https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/mercury-banking-built-for-startups) but it is a 2019 entry; no current award verified. Font MEM (Arcadia custom). Study: trustworthy fintech, light cool palette.
8. Daylight - V-typewolf SOTD 14 Dec 2025. Grenette (serif) + Styrene (sans). Domain unconfirmed. Study: serif + grotesk pair.
9. Muse - V-typewolf SOTD 11 Dec 2025. DaVinci (serif) + Suisse Int'l. Domain unconfirmed.
10. Dream Recorder - V-typewolf SOTD 22 Sep 2025. Times Now + Neue Montreal. Domain unconfirmed.
11. Pact - V-typewolf SOTD 28 Jun 2025. Manuka, Feature, Soehne. Domain unconfirmed.
12. Gradient - V-typewolf SOTD 26 Jun 2025. New Spirit (serif) + Outfit. Domain unconfirmed.

Not verified, MEM only (blue + product UI, good to check manually): linear.app, ramp.com, plain.com, cal.com, calendar.notion.so, paper.design, raycast.com, clerk.com, mintlify.com.

Gaps: WebSearch could not surface Awwwards SOTD/CSSDA/FWA winners by criteria; Awwwards pages gave no jury text. For award citations, browse https://www.awwwards.com/websites/sites_of_the_day/ and https://www.siteinspire.com manually.
Takeaway: Typewolf 2025 shows serif display + grotesk sans is the dominant pairing (Signifier/Switzer, Martina Plantijn/Inter, Tobias/Diatype). Fits our target.
