Questions

What is the LinkedIn Job Description Generator & JD Writer?

It is an AI LinkedIn JD writer and generator built specifically for recruiters, hiring managers, and startup founders. It turns brief intake notes into publication-ready LinkedIn job descriptions formatted with clean sections, bullet points, and equal-opportunity statements.

How do you keep the draft accurate?

The AI is told to use only what you provided, and its output must match a fixed structure. Pay numbers are added by code, never by the AI. Requirements that do not match your answers get a "verify" mark. Models are compared on the evaluation page. No AI is perfect, so read the draft before you post it.

Can I export my job description to Microsoft Word or Google Docs?

Yes. With one click on the "Export Word" button, you can download a formatted .docx file that opens perfectly in Microsoft Word, Apple Pages, or Google Docs.

Can I refine the generated draft?

Yes. One-click edits (shorter, more inclusive, simpler) and section rewrites are free. Typing your own instruction, such as "add a mentoring line", costs 1 credit and needs a free account.
