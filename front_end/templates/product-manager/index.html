Standard Product Manager Job Description

About [Company Name]

[Company Name] is a high-growth technology company on a mission to build products that empower modern teams. We value autonomy, rapid execution, and an inclusive culture where great ideas win.

About the Role

We are looking for a Product Manager to lead product discovery, drive roadmap execution, and deliver intuitive user experiences that fuel company growth.

Key Responsibilities

  • Define product strategy, roadmaps, and key performance metrics aligned with company goals.
  • Conduct continuous customer discovery, user interviews, and market research to uncover user pain points.
  • Author clear Product Requirement Documents (PRDs) and user stories for engineering and design teams.
  • Prioritize sprint backlogs and facilitate agile rituals (planning, standups, retrospectives).
  • Analyze product usage data and funnel conversion metrics to guide iterative improvements.
  • Collaborate with marketing and sales to ensure successful go-to-market feature launches.

Required Qualifications

  • 3+ years of product management experience managing B2B or consumer SaaS products.
  • Demonstrated track record of launching successful features from zero to one.
  • Strong quantitative analytical skills with tools like Mixpanel, Amplitude, SQL, or Google Analytics.
  • Outstanding verbal and written communication skills with cross-functional leadership ability.
  • Empathy for users and deep passion for high-craft UX design.

Preferred Qualifications (Nice-to-Haves)

  • Technical background or engineering degree.
  • Experience with AI/GenAI product workflows and prompt engineering.
  • Familiarity with PLG (Product-Led Growth) monetization models.

Benefits & Perks

  • Top-tier compensation package with meaningful equity grant.
  • 100% employer-covered health, vision, and dental insurance.
  • Flexible remote-first culture with global co-working passes.
  • Generous parental leave and wellness allowance.
  • Annual team offsites in exciting international locations.

Equal Opportunity Employer

[Company Name] is an equal opportunity employer. We celebrate diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive environment for all employees regardless of race, religion, color, gender, sexual orientation, or background.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a Product Manager job description stand out on LinkedIn?

Clarity on autonomy, ownership, and the specific business problem the PM will solve. Highlighting company growth and direct access to leadership attracts senior talent.

How do you evaluate PM applicants effectively?

Look for measurable business outcomes in past roles (e.g., 'increased retention by 24%'), strong written communication, and structured product thinking.
