Standard Human Resources (HR) Manager Job Description

About [Company Name]

[Company Name] is a high-growth technology company on a mission to build products that empower modern teams. We value autonomy, rapid execution, and an inclusive culture where great ideas win.

About the Role

We are seeking an experienced HR Manager to oversee talent acquisition, cultivate employee engagement, manage compliance, and champion our inclusive workplace culture.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead full-cycle HR operations including employee onboarding, performance reviews, and offboarding.
  • Develop and execute talent acquisition strategies to attract top diverse talent across all departments.
  • Ensure compliance with federal, state, and local employment laws, policies, and reporting.
  • Administer employee compensation, health benefits, 401(k), and wellness programs.
  • Mediate employee relations issues and foster an open, transparent company culture.

Required Qualifications

  • 3+ years of progressive HR management experience, ideally in growing tech or startup environments.
  • Comprehensive knowledge of employment laws, HR compliance, and modern people operations.
  • Experience managing HRIS and payroll software (Gusto, Rippling, BambooHR, ADP).
  • Exceptional emotional intelligence, confidentiality, and conflict resolution skills.
  • Strong verbal and written communication with leadership teams.

Preferred Qualifications (Nice-to-Haves)

  • SHRM-CP, SHRM-SCP, or PHR certification.
  • Experience managing distributed or remote-first global teams.

Benefits & Perks

  • Competitive compensation package with equity options.
  • Comprehensive healthcare coverage (medical, dental, vision).
  • Flexible remote work culture.
  • Generous parental leave and childcare assistance.
  • Annual professional development and certification stipend.

Equal Opportunity Employer

[Company Name] is an equal opportunity employer. We celebrate diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive environment for all employees regardless of race, religion, color, gender, sexual orientation, or background.
