Standard Software Engineer Job Description

About [Company Name]

[Company Name] is a high-growth technology company on a mission to build products that empower modern teams. We value autonomy, rapid execution, and an inclusive culture where great ideas win.

About the Role

We are seeking a talented Software Engineer to design, build, and scale high-impact features across our web platform and microservices.

Key Responsibilities

  • Architect, build, and maintain scalable web applications and RESTful APIs.
  • Collaborate closely with product managers, designers, and frontend engineers to ship high-quality features.
  • Write clean, well-tested, and maintainable code adhering to engineering best practices.
  • Optimize backend database queries, application latency, and system throughput.
  • Participate in code reviews, design docs, and architectural discussions.
  • Diagnose, debug, and resolve production issues with minimal downtime.

Required Qualifications

  • 2+ years of professional software engineering experience in full-stack or backend development.
  • Proficiency in modern programming languages such as Python, TypeScript, Go, or Java.
  • Hands-on experience with relational databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL) and ORMs.
  • Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS, Google Cloud, or Azure) and containerization (Docker).
  • Strong understanding of software engineering fundamentals (data structures, algorithms, system design).
  • Excellent problem-solving skills and effective team communication.

Preferred Qualifications (Nice-to-Haves)

  • Experience with modern frontend frameworks like React, Next.js, or Vue.
  • Prior background with LLM integration, OpenAI API, or AI-assisted tooling.
  • Experience working in fast-paced startup or agile environments.

Benefits & Perks

  • Competitive salary with equity / stock options.
  • Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance.
  • Flexible work arrangements (Remote or Hybrid).
  • Unlimited paid time off (PTO) and company holidays.
  • $1,500 annual learning & conference stipend.
  • Home office setup reimbursement.

Equal Opportunity Employer

[Company Name] is an equal opportunity employer. We celebrate diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive environment for all employees regardless of race, religion, color, gender, sexual orientation, or background.

Frequently Asked Questions

What skills should be prioritized in a Software Engineer job description?

Focus on core language proficiency (e.g. Python, TypeScript), system design understanding, testing discipline, and experience with modern deployment workflows (Docker, CI/CD).

How long should a LinkedIn Software Engineer job post be?

Keep it between 300 and 500 words. Software engineers prefer concise, bullet-pointed descriptions that clearly highlight the tech stack, salary range, and company mission.

Should I include the tech stack in the job title?

Yes! Adding keywords like 'Software Engineer (Python / React)' or 'Full Stack Engineer' helps people find the post when they search for the role.
