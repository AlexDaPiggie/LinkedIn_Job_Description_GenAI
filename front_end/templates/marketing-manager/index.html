Standard Marketing Manager Job Description

About [Company Name]

[Company Name] is a high-growth technology company on a mission to build products that empower modern teams. We value autonomy, rapid execution, and an inclusive culture where great ideas win.

About the Role

We are looking for a creative and data-driven Marketing Manager to spearhead our multi-channel marketing campaigns, boost inbound leads, and elevate brand awareness.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and execute end-to-end inbound and outbound marketing strategies to generate qualified leads.
  • Manage digital advertising campaigns across LinkedIn Ads, Google Search, and paid social channels.
  • Oversee content marketing, SEO strategy, case studies, and email newsletter workflows.
  • Analyze campaign ROI, customer acquisition cost (CAC), and pipeline contribution.
  • Partner with sales and product teams to refine brand messaging, value propositions, and sales collateral.

Required Qualifications

  • 3+ years of marketing management experience in B2B SaaS or technology companies.
  • Demonstrated track record of scaling inbound lead acquisition and web traffic.
  • Hands-on expertise with marketing automation platforms (HubSpot, Marketo) and Google Analytics 4.
  • Strong copywriting skills with keen eye for brand aesthetics and messaging consistency.
  • Data-driven mindset with experience managing advertising budgets and calculating ROI.

Preferred Qualifications (Nice-to-Haves)

  • Experience organizing webinars, podcasts, or community marketing events.
  • Basic design ability in Canva, Figma, or Adobe Creative Suite.
  • Knowledge of Product-Led Growth (PLG) marketing tactics.

Benefits & Perks

  • Competitive salary with performance bonuses.
  • Comprehensive health benefits.
  • Remote-first work environment.
  • Generous vacation policy and mental health days.
  • Marketing tech stack and creative experimentation budget.

Equal Opportunity Employer

[Company Name] is an equal opportunity employer. We celebrate diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive environment for all employees regardless of race, religion, color, gender, sexual orientation, or background.
