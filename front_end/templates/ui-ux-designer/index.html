Standard UI/UX Designer Job Description

About [Company Name]

[Company Name] is a high-growth technology company on a mission to build products that empower modern teams. We value autonomy, rapid execution, and an inclusive culture where great ideas win.

About the Role

We are seeking a talented UI/UX Designer to craft beautiful, intuitive web and mobile experiences that solve complex user problems and elevate our brand identity.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead end-to-end product design from wireframes, user flows, and interactive Figma prototypes to final UI.
  • Build, scale, and maintain our multi-platform design system and component libraries.
  • Conduct usability testing, user interviews, and competitive design audits to inform design decisions.
  • Partner closely with product managers and engineers to ensure high-fidelity implementation.
  • Advocate for user-centric design principles, accessibility (WCAG), and responsive layouts.

Required Qualifications

  • 3+ years of product design (UI/UX) experience with a strong online portfolio showcasing shipped digital products.
  • Expert-level mastery of Figma, auto-layout, component variants, and interactive prototyping.
  • Deep understanding of typography, color theory, spacing, and micro-interactions.
  • Proven ability to translate complex technical workflows into simple, elegant interfaces.
  • Strong communication skills to articulate design rationales to cross-functional teams.

Preferred Qualifications (Nice-to-Haves)

  • Basic understanding of HTML/CSS and frontend layout constraints.
  • Experience with motion design, micro-animations, or design systems documentation (Zeroheight, Storybook).

Benefits & Perks

  • Top compensation, equity, and performance bonuses.
  • Top-of-the-line design workstation (MacBook Pro + Studio Display).
  • Full health, dental, and vision insurance.
  • Figma and design conference budget.
  • Flexible remote work schedule.

Equal Opportunity Employer

[Company Name] is an equal opportunity employer. We celebrate diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive environment for all employees regardless of race, religion, color, gender, sexual orientation, or background.
