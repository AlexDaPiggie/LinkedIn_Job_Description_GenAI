About [Company Name]

[Company Name] is a high-growth technology company on a mission to build products that empower modern teams. We value autonomy, rapid execution, and an inclusive culture where great ideas win.

About the Role

We are looking for a detail-oriented Data Analyst to turn raw business data into actionable dashboards, reports, and strategic recommendations.

Key Responsibilities

  • Write complex SQL queries to extract, transform, and analyze operational and customer data.
  • Build and maintain automated BI dashboards in Tableau, PowerBI, or Metabase for leadership.
  • Track core business KPIs across sales, marketing, and product retention funnels.
  • Conduct ad-hoc exploratory analyses to uncover trends, anomalies, and revenue opportunities.
  • Collaborate with engineering to define tracking specs and data governance standards.

Required Qualifications

  • 2+ years of professional experience as a Data Analyst or Business Intelligence Analyst.
  • Advanced SQL proficiency (window functions, CTEs, query optimization).
  • Proven track record creating intuitive dashboards with Tableau, PowerBI, or Looker.
  • Strong communication skills with the ability to explain numbers to non-technical stakeholders.
  • Proficiency with Excel (pivot tables, complex formulas) and introductory Python/R.

Preferred Qualifications (Nice-to-Haves)

  • Familiarity with modern data stack tools like dbt, Fivetran, and Snowflake.
  • Experience in SaaS metrics (LTV, CAC, MRR, churn rate).

Benefits & Perks

  • Competitive salary and performance bonuses.
  • Health, dental, vision coverage.
  • Remote work flexibility.
  • Learning & development budget for certifications.
  • Generous PTO and paid parental leave.
