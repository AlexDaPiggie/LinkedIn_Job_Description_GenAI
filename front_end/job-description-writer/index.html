What the wording check looks for

  • Gender-coded words. Terms like "dominant" or "fearless" that research links to fewer applicants from some groups.
  • Age-coded words. Phrases like "digital native" or "young and dynamic".
  • Filler and hype. "Rockstar", "ninja", "synergy" and other words that say little.
  • Missing pay. Posts with a pay range tend to get more applicants, and some places require it.
  • Long bullet lists. More than 8 duties or requirements lowers applications. Keep the real ones.
  • Reading level. Long, dense sentences that are hard to scan on a phone.
  • Unverified requirements. Anything the AI added that is not in your answers.
  • Missing sections. A broken or incomplete draft is caught before you see it.

The check is a screening aid for wording and structure, not legal advice.

Tips for a job description people actually apply to

  1. Lead with the work, not the company history. Candidates decide in the first few lines.
  2. Be specific. "Own the onboarding flow for 200 new B2B customers a month" beats "drive customer success".
  3. Separate must-haves from nice-to-haves. Many qualified people skip a post if they miss one listed requirement.
  4. Show the pay range. It saves both sides time and is required by law in a growing number of places.
  5. Name the tools and the team. Stack, team size and who they report to answer the questions candidates ask first.
  6. Keep it scannable. Short bullets, plain words, under about 700 words for most roles.

The writer asks for exactly these facts, so the draft follows these tips by default.

Frequently asked questions

What is an AI job description writer?

It is a tool that writes a full job description from a few facts about the role. You give the title, duties, must-have skills and benefits. It returns a structured draft with an intro, responsibilities, qualifications, benefits and an equal opportunity statement.

Is this job description writer free?

Yes. Writing a full job description, the wording check, quick edits, copying to LinkedIn and Word export are free with no account. Only custom refine requests, where you type your own change, use credits. A free account gets 20 credits.

How is this different from asking ChatGPT?

ChatGPT writes whatever you prompt. This writer asks for the facts a job post needs, keeps the same section structure every time, never invents pay numbers, marks requirements that were not in your answers, and runs a rule-based check for gender-coded and age-coded wording, filler words and missing pay.

Will the AI make up requirements or salary?

The AI never writes pay numbers. If you add a pay range it appears exactly as you typed it. If the draft lists a requirement that shares no words with your answers, it gets a verify mark so you can check or remove it.

Can I use it for job boards other than LinkedIn?

Yes. The output is plain, well-structured text. It is formatted for LinkedIn, but it works on Indeed, your careers page or any job board. You can also download it as a Word file.

Can I rewrite an old job description?

Yes. Upload your existing job description file and the tool fills in the intake questions from it. Adjust the answers, then write a fresh draft.
