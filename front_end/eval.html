How the test works

  • Scenarios

    Four fixed hiring cases

    Only the required fields, every field filled, a mix of fields, and a tricky edge case. Each also has a refinement request.

  • Rule checks

    Is the output usable?

    It must be valid JSON that matches the required structure, include the facts it was given, and leave out facts it was not given.

  • Quality score

    A second AI model judges

    A model from a family we did not test rates specificity, tone, faithfulness to the input, LinkedIn-readiness and how well the refinement was applied, from 1 to 5.

  • Speed and cost

    Measured on every call

    Timings and token counts come from the real calls, and cost uses the OpenRouter list price on a fixed date.
