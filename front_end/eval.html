How the test works

  • Four fixed hiring scenarios: only the required fields, every field filled, a mix of fields, and a tricky edge case. Each has a refinement request too.
  • Rule checks: the output must be valid JSON that matches the required structure, include the facts it was given, and leave out facts it was not given.
  • Quality scores: a second AI model (the judge) rates specificity, tone, faithfulness to the input, LinkedIn-readiness and how well the refinement was applied, from 1 to 5.
  • Cost and speed: measured on every call from the real token counts and timings.

How to read this

  • Overall is an average of specificity, tone, faithfulness and LinkedIn-readiness scores from an LLM judge, scale 1 to 5.
  • Faithful means the draft does not add facts the user never gave.
  • Schema pass is the share of runs whose JSON matched the required structure.
  • Cost / call is the estimated token cost of one generate or refine call.

Limits: a small scenario set, and an LLM judge can be biased. Treat gaps under about 0.1 as ties. Results reflect the models and prompts at the time they were run.
