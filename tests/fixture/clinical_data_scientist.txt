=======================================================
Company: BioVance Therapeutics
Location: Boston, MA (Hybrid)

1. ROLE SUMMARY:
BioVance Therapeutics is seeking a skilled Clinical Data Scientist to lead statistical analysis of phase I/II clinical trial datasets, develop predictive biomarker pipelines, and support FDA submission dossiers.

2. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Analyze multi-omics and patient electronic health records (EHR) from ongoing oncology clinical trials.
- Develop and validate machine learning models for patient stratification and treatment response prediction.
- Collaborate with medical oncologists, clinical trial managers, and biostatisticians to generate clinical study reports.
- Ensure strict adherence to CDISC SDTM and ADaM clinical data formatting standards.
- Prepare regulatory data summaries and presentation decks for FDA and EMA advisory meetings.

3. CANDIDATE REQUIREMENTS:
- Ph.D. or Master's degree in Bioinformatics, Biostatistics, Computational Biology, or related quantitative field.
- 3+ years experience analyzing clinical trial or real-world evidence (RWE) healthcare data.
- Expert programming skills in Python (scikit-learn, pandas) and R (Bioconductor, survival analysis).
- Solid understanding of GCP (Good Clinical Practice) and HIPAA compliance requirements.

4. PREFERRED / NICE-TO-HAVE:
- Familiarity with deep learning architectures applied to digital pathology or survival forecasting.
- Proven track record of peer-reviewed publications in clinical oncology or bioinformatics journals.
- Experience with cloud-based healthcare data warehouses such as Snowflake or AWS HealthLake.

5. ABOUT THE COMPANY:

6. WHY JOIN US:
